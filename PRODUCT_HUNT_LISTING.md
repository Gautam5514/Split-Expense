## First comment (maker)
Hi Product Hunt 👋 I'm Kunal, maker of SplitEase.

Splitting a trip bill usually means a spreadsheet, a dozen "who owes what" texts and someone forgetting to pay. SplitEase replaces that with one flow: create a group, log the expense (or scan the receipt), and let the app net everything into the fewest possible payments.

What's inside:
- 🧮 4 split modes: equal, %, shares, exact
- 🧭 Autopilot: type "rent 20000" or "dinner 3000 Rahul ko chhod ke" (English/Hinglish). It drafts a plan and changes nothing until you approve.
- 📸 AI receipt scanner (OCR → itemized expense)
- ⚡ Minimum-transfer settle-up + one-tap UPI, confirmed by the person being paid
- 💬 Realtime chat and ledger in one thread
- 🛰️ Remote MCP server with 29 tools (OAuth 2.1 + PKCE) so Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor can create groups, add bills and settle up – same rules as the app
- 🔓 Open source (MIT), 800+ automated tests

Live on Google Play with 10,000+ travelers. I'd love your feedback – especially on Autopilot and the MCP connector. What would you want your AI assistant to do with your shared expenses?
